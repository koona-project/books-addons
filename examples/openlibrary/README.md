# Open Library

Finds information about a book, and covers for it, at openlibrary.org: a free catalogue of books in
many languages, kept by the Internet Archive.

## What it does

- Looks a book up when you choose Find information or Find cover: by its ISBN when the book has one,
  otherwise by its title and its first author.
- Answers in the language of the book first, then in the languages you read in.
- Gives the edition it found: title, authors, publisher, year, ISBN, and a cover.
- Gives a description only for an edition in English: Open Library writes its descriptions in
  English, and a book is never described in a language that is not its own.

## What it may do

It may reach openlibrary.org, covers.openlibrary.org and archive.org (where Open Library keeps its
pictures), and nothing else. The title, the authors and the ISBN of the book you look up go to
openlibrary.org, only when you ask. It never sees a book file, your library or anything else on your
computer. Koona Books fetches the pictures itself and checks each one before it is shown.

## What it does not do

- Change anything by itself. You see what was found beside what the library has and tick what to
  take.
- Rename an author into another script, or translate a title.
- Give tags: Open Library's subjects are a mixed list in English.
- Give the title a translated book was first printed under: Open Library does not say which it is.

## How it was checked

Against openlibrary.org by hand on 12 November 2026: a search by title and author in Russian, a
search by ISBN, a work's record and a cover. A catalogue changes; when something stops working, the
add-on is updated.
