# Xteink memory card

Lets Koona Books send books to the memory card of an Xteink X4: take the card out of the reader, put
it into your computer's card reader, and send to it as to any e-reader.

## What it does

- Tells Koona Books what the card of an Xteink X4 running CrossPoint looks like: it has a
  `.crosspoint` folder. Put such a card into the computer and it shows up under Devices by itself.
- Says what the reader opens: EPUB and TXT. Other books are converted to EPUB by Koona Books first.
- Puts each book in a folder named after its author.
- Never touches the `.crosspoint` folder, where the reader keeps its own settings.

## What it may do

Nothing of it runs: it is a description of the reader, read by Koona Books. It reaches no network and
no file on your computer. Koona Books copies the books to the card itself.

## Needs

An Xteink X4 running CrossPoint, and a way to put its memory card into your computer. To send without
taking the card out, use CrossPoint over Wi-Fi.

## How it was checked

By Koona Books' own tests on 12 November 2026, which read this description under the rules for anyone's
add-on. It has not yet been tried with a reader's card in a computer.
