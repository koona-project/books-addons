# Where these folders come from

`examples/`, `starters/`, `skill/`, `schema/`, and `withdrawn.json` are a copy of what Koona Books publishes for add-on authors at its commit `17f1cbf6`, taken 2026-11-21. They are not edited here: a change is made in Koona Books and copied again (`bun run addons:docs --sync`), so that what is read here is what the app ships and what the documents at https://addme.dev/books/docs/ describe.
