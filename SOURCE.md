# Where these folders come from

`examples/`, `starters/` and `skill/` are a copy of what Koona Books publishes for add-on authors at its commit `9b98615`, taken 2026-11-13. They are not edited here: a change is made in Koona Books and copied again, so that what is read here is what the app ships and what the documents at https://addons.koona.app/books/docs/ describe.
