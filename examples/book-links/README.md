# Book links

Adds Find on Hardcover and Find on Open Library to a book's menu. Each opens the site's search for the
book in your browser.

## What it does

- Searches by the book's ISBN when it has one: that finds the very edition.
- Otherwise searches by the title and the first author.
- Says so when the book has neither an ISBN nor a title.

## What it may do

It reaches no network and no file on your computer. It makes a link from the book's title, author and
ISBN, and Koona Books opens that link in your browser, only when you click, and only if it leads to
hardcover.app or openlibrary.org. What is in the link goes to that site when your browser opens it.

## How it was checked

By Koona Books' own tests on 12 November 2026, with books in English and in Russian, with and without
an ISBN. The search pages themselves have not yet been opened by hand.
